Keeping Your Olympia HVAC System Running Longer

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and schedule a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, a central air conditioner usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like rising energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's smart to start planning a replacement. A planned replacement is cheaper and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new system during the first heat wave of the year.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor left alone for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
